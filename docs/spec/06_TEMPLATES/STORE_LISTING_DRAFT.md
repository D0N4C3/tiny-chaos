## Full description draft

Every tiny machine is about to go wrong. One clever move could save the day!

Meet Pip, the little inventor robot with spectacularly bad luck. Place springs, flip switches, control magnets and start ridiculous chain reactions in a colorful toy-sized world. Rescue Pip, discover unexpected solutions and laugh at delightfully silly failures.

- Solve 120 handcrafted physics puzzle levels across six whimsical worlds.
- Play short Fix It, Save It and Chaos Combo challenges.
- Rewind mistakes for free and try a better solution.
- Discover optional mastery medals and create a miniature invention workshop.
- Try new Daily Circuit puzzles and themed weekly challenges.
- Challenge friends to solve the same machine your way.

Designed for short, rewarding play sessions. Play the campaign offline. Optional ads and purchases are available; normal puzzle retries and rewind remain free.

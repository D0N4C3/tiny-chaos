# Organic growth, store listing and postlaunch live operations

## Commercial hypothesis to test

An accessible single-decision physics puzzle can earn installs through a clear visual hook, while real-time intervention, free checkpoint rewind, alternate medals and curated friend challenges can support repeat play. **Neither virality nor retention is proven by this documentation.**

## Organic growth plan

1. **ASO:** Try exact honest metadata themes: `physics puzzle`, `chain reaction`, `rescue`, `brain puzzle`, `funny toy machine`, `one-tap puzzle`. Do not keyword-stuff title. Choose name after search and trademark screening.
2. **Short-form content:** Record 20 real in-game clips, 8–15 seconds each; test question-opening / funny failure / clever route independently. Always show attainable game state.
3. **Community:** Seek feedback in relevant puzzle/game development groups in compliance with rules; solicit genuine feedback, not fake review swaps or undisclosed paid endorsements.
4. **Share cards:** In-app 'Beat My Rescue' should generate trackable, meaningful participation and not spam contacts or require permission to contacts.
5. **Cross-promotion:** If owner has another app and consent/policy allows, use tasteful cross-promotions and avoid misleading audiences.
6. **Creative learning:** Compare CTR → install conversion → D1 retention → D7 retention → LTV, not views and likes alone.

## Store assets and claims

Per Google Play's preview asset specification (verified 2026-10-29): store app icon 512×512 px 32-bit PNG, feature graphic 1024×500 px JPEG/24-bit PNG, and truthful screenshots from shipped build. Current source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151

Avoid using screenshots that include visual effects or mechanics unavailable in shipped release. Read current screenshot counts/aspect constraints directly in Play Console at submission.

## First 12 weeks of liveops

Use `WEEKLY_EVENT_PLAN_12.csv` as the rotating calendar. Before each weekly event, QA the exact production build and content ID, verify event assets and rewards, then enable the episode. Prepare a 30-day replacement daily schedule with validated remixes well before the first rotation repeats.

## Feedback-driven level fixes

- Daily dashboard: app open rate, new installs, D1/D7 by cohort, level exits, repeated failed attempts, time to win, `Next` tap rate, reward offer opt-in, ad impression cap distribution, IAP errors.
- Weekly priority review: fix levels with abnormal exit rate, ambiguous failure cause, or collision nondeterminism ahead of producing more content.
- Release notes must be truthful, short and localized; ship fixes faster than new complex features.

## Postlaunch feature candidates (NOT in v1 full release)

- Curated guest creator challenge packs after licensing and QA.
- Moderated user-generated puzzle sharing only with reporting/moderation capacity.
- Optional cloud save and signed leaderboards after server cost/privacy review.
- New themed world expansions (no hard dependency for initial release).

## KPI gates (provisional, not predictions)

D1 30–35%+, D7 8–12%+, D30 >3%; clear first level under 45s; ≥60% willing to continue from level 5; >70% of returning players understand daily system without explanation. Revisit targets when real cohort sizes and confidence bounds support decisions. Paid acquisition requires actual ROAS/LTV evidence.
